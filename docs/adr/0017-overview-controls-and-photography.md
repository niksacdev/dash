# 0017. Give the Overview controls and photography a shared visual hierarchy

**Status** Accepted

During a walkthrough, the theme and slideshow controls competed with **New run**. The industry slide also replaced the circuit’s red-lit background with slate cards. Nik requested neutral presentation controls, blended images and continuity as the slides advance.

Keep the action palette from [0007](0007-racing-yellow-actions.md) for workflow actions. Give the appearance switch a bordered neutral selection and the slideshow a single graphite control surface. Both have a visible keyboard focus indicator. Match the device power readout to the adjacent telemetry values. Keep the six slides on the same graphite-to-garnet gradient. The circuit fades out for the industry slide. Local CSS masks soften the photograph edges while the central subject and captions stay readable. This extends [0015](0015-industry-carousel.md).

A different color treatment for every slide would make transitions more conspicuous. Tinting each entire photograph would obscure the paper, metal and paint that distinguish the three domains. Keep the original images intact so the crops and fades can adapt to the viewport.

Verify the control colors in both themes through Electron. Review the slide at phone, intermediate and desktop widths, with pause, keyboard navigation and reduced motion. [Design guide and captured views](../design/README.md)
