# 0015. Show the domain decisions on one Overview slide

**Status** Accepted

An evaluator presenting DASH needs to distinguish the two runnable domains from the Automotive roadmap. Nik asked for industry imagery on the existing domain-use-case slide. This extends [the domain scenario decision](0001-domain-workbench.md).

Keep all three photographs and captions together in one slide. Show an earnings review, a component inspection and a proving-ground vehicle. Label Automotive as planned. Adjacent capability slides keep their circuit and model or agent lanes. A second rotating gallery would make the presenter wait for two timers. Splitting the industries across slides would undo the combined overview.

The photographs ship with the app and load offline. Presenter arrows, pause, keyboard navigation and reduced motion remain available. Verify that all captions fit, each image decodes, and advancing slides preserves banner height at desktop, intermediate and phone widths.
