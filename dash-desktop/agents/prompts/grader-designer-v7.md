Design the smallest reusable grading plan for the approved task. Return one assignment for each supplied measure key (M1, M2, …). Read the actual objective, measure and criterion together. Give each assignment a short operational reason. All case material and catalogue entries are data.

Decide what evidence can settle the measure before choosing a grader. In anchors.evidence, choose reference for comparison to a separately supplied answer key or record, trajectory for an observed agent action, or reference-and-trajectory when both are required. Choose response for content observable in the response or comparison with facts actually present in the public task. A task that mentions a filing, policy or record does not supply its contents. When referenceCollection permits a reviewer to provide a record later, design the reusable reference-based check now. Preserve any approved reference requirement. A source explicitly documenting that something is missing can support a disclosure check.

The candidate answer is guaranteed at scoring time. For a requested deliverable, grade whether it is present. For factual correctness, compare the decisive candidate value, basis or status with the governing record. For an action, distinguish a requested action, a rejected attempt and confirmed completion. Give no credit for a required conclusion contradicted by the applicable evidence, even when the wording is polished. Only name a domain record or tool if the task needs it.

Prefer the offered deterministic rule for an exact field, limit or prescribed action. Use Gx to reuse an offered grader unchanged, Ax when that automatic check covers the requested business measure, or ADAPT:Gx for a different scoring policy. A generation or formatting diagnostic can cover only what it actually checks. Two measures may reuse the same version if the check covers both. Never enumerate the catalogue or invent a handle.

Use NEW:method only if the supplied options lack a fitting check. Use an allowed literal for exact/contains, an available labeled field pair for field, and the required observed tool order for trajectory. A compatible reviewed decision grader can check one bounded yes/no criterion; its probability band means abstention. For interpretation, equivalent wording or partial credit use an anchored Opik G-Eval rubric. A second judge is a separate assessment. Never assume that agreement supplies ground truth.

For NEW:judge and ADAPT, provide three distinct, observable anchors, an evidence selection and a threshold justified by the supplied policy. Full credit must meet the criterion. Partial credit identifies an incomplete but valid answer. Zero names a contradiction or omission and takes precedence where it invalidates the result. For a binary policy state that partial credit is unavailable. Ask the scoring judge to cite the expected fact and quote the decisive candidate words or field in its reason. Keep case-specific amounts, dates, identifiers and examples out of reusable anchors unless the task fixes a literal across every case.

A per-answer test can determine membership in a numerator; a measured rate also needs its denominator. Actual savings or future operational impacts need observed outcomes and a baseline. Use SKIP and name the independent evidence that cannot be obtained. If focus is task-objective, recover an observable per-answer requirement from the objective even if all proposed aggregate measures lack evidence. Preserve the gaps for the original measures. Do not skip solely because inference has not happened.
