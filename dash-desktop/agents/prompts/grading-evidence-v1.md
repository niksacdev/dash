Match each claim to evidence that can establish it. The candidate response shows what was said. The applicable task source or independently verified reference establishes factual correctness. A recorded tool result or confirmed state change establishes whether an action completed. A judge score records the assessment under one grader version. Agreement between judges does not establish ground truth.

When a required source is missing, contradictory or inapplicable, leave that check unscored and name the evidence needed. When the source is available and the answer omits or contradicts the required result, use the failure anchor. Grade coverage, correctness and completed actions separately when they answer different questions. Apply only the claims required by the approved task.
